# SEO assessment

Updated: 2026-09-29

## Implemented local foundations

The tracked source contains stable Texas event, city, and trail routes; page-specific metadata and canonicals; internal navigation; breadcrumbs; structured data; source attribution; and sitemap inclusion guarded by explicit public-indexing configuration.

## Material launch gate

Indexability must remain disabled until a verified canonical HTTPS domain is attached and the site has truthful operator, contact, privacy, terms, retention, and deletion information. A local build, test run, saved Worker version, or `workers.dev` endpoint is not evidence of a public SEO launch.

## Required release verification

After a separately authorized deployment, inspect representative public pages, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` over the canonical origin. Confirm canonical URLs, `index, follow` only where intended, noindex for APIs/accounts/diagnostics, sitemap coverage, and deployed structured-data output before requesting indexing or submitting a sitemap.
