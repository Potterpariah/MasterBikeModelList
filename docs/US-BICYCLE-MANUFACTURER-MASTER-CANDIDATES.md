# U.S. bicycle manufacturer master candidate index — Candidate Index v1

Status: **frozen Candidate Index v1**, September 30, 2026. This is a broad discovery list for brands that are known, reported, or plausibly findable as current or historical bicycle brands in the U.S. market from 1990 onward. It is **not a verified all-manufacturer census** and it makes no claim that every brand sold every listed year in the U.S.

## v1 audit metadata

- Version: `candidate-index-v1`
- Frozen on: 2026-09-30
- Normalized canonical candidates before audit: **530**
- Added canonical candidates in this audit: **15**
- Normalized canonical candidates after audit: **545**
- Displayed research-queue labels may repeat across discipline queues. They are aliases/category cross-references, not additional canonical candidates; the count above is case-insensitive and normalized.
- This freeze is a discovery boundary, not authorization to import, scrape, publish, migrate, index, or assert a complete U.S. census.

## Rules

- A name becomes a verified manufacturer record only after an official U.S. page, official catalog/manual/archive, or documented U.S. distributor evidence is added.
- A name may have been a brand, importer, private label, or acquired/discontinued marque; ownership, manufacturing location, and U.S. sale dates must not be inferred from the name.
- The list excludes component-only and accessory-only brands unless they sold complete bicycles under the same brand.
- Candidate names must be normalized and deduplicated case-insensitively before a manufacturer record is created. A familiar brand may appear in more than one research queue here to make manual review easier; category labels are not proof of product range.

## Audit exclusions and reconciliation notes

- **Aliases, not additions:** `Bottecchia` (not Bottechia), `GoCycle` (not Gocycle), `TerraTrike` (not Terratrike), `Scott` (not Scott USA), and `Diamondback` (not Diamond Back) remain canonical spellings. `Townie` remains an Electra model label, not a candidate manufacturer.
- **Corporate/parent names, not brand additions:** American Bicycle Group and Accell are not added in this pass as separate bicycle-brand candidates merely from group identity; their actual branded bicycles require their own evidence.
- **Component-only exclusions from v1 additions:** Bike Index marks examples such as DVO, ENVE, Formula, and Profile Racing as component-only. They are not bicycle-brand additions. A Bike Index blank is only a lead, never sufficient by itself to establish U.S. market presence.
- **Rejected as unsupported global-only additions:** no candidate was added solely because it appeared in a global directory, retailer directory, or a wholesale listing without complete-bicycle and plausible U.S.-market evidence.

## Evidence sources and limitations

- [PeopleForBikes](https://www.peopleforbikes.org/news/why-become-a-peopleforbikes-member) reports 320+ bicycle-brand coalition members; it establishes that the U.S. industry is much larger than a short “popular brands” list, but it is not a historical manufacturer census.
- [Bike Index’s manufacturer directory](https://bikeindex.org/manufacturers) is a useful discovery lead and points to a CSV, but its dataset is separately licensed. Its component-only column was used only to exclude obvious component-only leads; neither its CSV nor its data was copied into this repository. The catalog architecture continues to treat Bike Index data as ODbL-restricted research, not an import source.
- [The Pro’s Closet brand directory](https://www.theproscloset.com/pages/brands) is a retailer discovery source that helped identify current/performance candidates. It is not evidence that every listed brand was sold in the U.S. in every year.
- [PeopleForBikes’ E-Bike Smart supporters](https://www.peopleforbikes.org/news/introducing-e-bike-smart-a-new-rider-education-program) provide a dated U.S.-industry lead for several e-bike candidates, including Bulls, IZIP, QuietKat, Urban Arrow, Velotric, Yamaha, and Yuba.
- [The Pro’s Closet U.S.-made brand guide](https://www.theproscloset.com/blogs/news/american-made-bikes) was used only to broaden the boutique/custom queue; its current-status statements require later confirmation from each maker.
- [Bicycle Retailer’s 2019 Industry Directory](https://www.bicycleretailer.com/sites/default/files/industry_directory_2019.pdf) is a dated distributor-directory discovery lead for brands such as 9:ZERO:7, Avian, Speedco, Del Sol, Gazelle, Haro, Masi, Stromer, and other candidates. It is not treated as proof of a complete product line, precise U.S. sale period, or current availability.
- [The Pro’s Closet DTC guide](https://www.theproscloset.com/blogs/news/a-guide-to-direct-to-consumer-bike-brands) is an additional discovery lead for direct-sales brands. Its 2022 publication date means every active-status claim must be refreshed before public use.
- [Trek/Electra historical archive](https://electra.trekbikes.com/us/en_US/historical-bike-archive/) explicitly supports Trek and Gary Fisher archive discovery for 2002–2009.
- Official archives documented in `US-HISTORICAL-BIKE-CATALOG-REGISTER.md` are the preferred sources for creating actual model-year records.
