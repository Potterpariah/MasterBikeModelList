## Verification rule applied

Each record has an official U.S. page, satisfying the discovery-to-manufacturer identity gate. Exact year/model/trim data remains out of scope until an official U.S. product page, catalog, manual, or archive proves that exact row. Region-specific archives above are retained only as research pointers and are not a substitute for U.S. availability.
