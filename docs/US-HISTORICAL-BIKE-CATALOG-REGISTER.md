# U.S. bicycle manufacturer historical register

## Scope and truthfulness boundary

Target: bicycles marketed in the United States from 1990 onward.

"All manufacturers" cannot be asserted as a completed finite list: there is no public, authoritative master register covering every active, defunct, private-label, direct-to-consumer, and imported brand sold in the U.S. during that period. This register is therefore cumulative. A manufacturer enters only when a source can be recorded; a manufacturer-year-model entry enters only when an official manufacturer page, catalog, manual, or archive supports it.

This is manual historical documentation, not automated ingestion. The public record stores factual identifiers (manufacturer, category/type, model family, model year, named model/trim, source URL, source type, market, and review date). It does not copy product photography, marketing copy, geometry diagrams, or proprietary database exports.

## Research order

1. Manufacturer official U.S. page/archive/certificate of U.S. distribution.
2. Official manufacturer annual catalog or manual for the exact model year.
3. Official region archive only as corroboration, labeled with its market.
4. Manufacturer/distributor confirmation for an unresolved U.S. sale question.

No secondary database is used to fill missing year/model fields. A missing field remains missing.
